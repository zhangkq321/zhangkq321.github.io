I am a Ph.D. candidate at the **College of Civil Engineering, Tongji University**, Shanghai, China.

My research focuses on computational geomechanics and rock engineering, in particular the **numerical manifold method (NMM)** and its element-free / meshfree variants, **discontinuous deformation analysis (DDA)**, and their application to jointed rock masses, tunnel stability, fracture propagation and heat conduction in cracked materials.

**Selected publications** are listed below; the full list is on the [publications page](publications/).

- **2017--2021** B.Eng. in Engineering Management, Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology
